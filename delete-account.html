Delete your account

How to remove your dishd account, what happens to your recipes, photos and profile, and what we keep afterwards — nothing, unless the law requires it.

Last updated: 20 November 2026 Version: 1.0 Data contact: contact@dishd.pl

In short: Profile → Settings → Delete account deletes everything immediately and the app returns to guest mode. Without the app, one e-mail from your registered address is enough — we delete the account within 7 days. Deletion is permanent.

1. Delete it in the app

This is the fastest route and it needs no e-mail, no waiting and no confirmation from us.

  1. Open dishd and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Go to Profile and open Settings (the gear icon).
  3. At the bottom choose Delete account and confirm the dialog.
  4. Your account and content are erased immediately and the app returns to guest mode.

The deletion starts the moment you confirm — there is no recovery window. If you close the dialog without confirming, nothing changes and your account stays as it was.

2. Delete it without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, write from the e-mail address registered to your account to contact@dishd.pl with the subject “Delete my dishd account”. We delete the account within 7 days and confirm it by e-mail. If you write from a different address, we will ask you to confirm the account so that nobody else can delete it.

Write to contact@dishd.pl

3. What we delete

Deleting the account removes everything connected to it:

  • the account itself — e-mail, password hash and Google or Apple sign-in data;
  • nick, bio, profile photo, banner and website link;
  • all your recipes, comments, ratings, photos and videos;
  • likes, saved recipes, collections, the shopping list and your cooked marks;
  • follows, blocks, push notification tokens and reports you sent;
  • dietary preferences and privacy settings.

4. Backups and what happens next

Your active data is removed straight away. Encrypted backups are overwritten within 30 days and are not used for anything else in the meantime. We do not keep your data after deletion, unless the law requires us to.

  • Deletion is permanent — the account cannot be restored, and the same e-mail can be used for a new account later.
  • Content other people saved, screenshot or shared while your account existed is outside our control.
  • E-mails you exchanged with us are kept only as long as needed to handle the matter and are then removed.

5. Export a copy first, if you want one

Deletion cannot be undone, so if you would like to keep your recipes, export them before you delete the account: Settings → Privacy and security → Download my data. The export arrives by e-mail and is yours to keep.

6. Something not working?

If the app refuses to delete the account, if you cannot sign in to delete it yourself, or if you simply want us to do it for you, write to contact@dishd.pl. A real person answers, usually within a couple of days.

More detail on your rights and on how we handle personal data is in the GDPR page and the privacy policy.
