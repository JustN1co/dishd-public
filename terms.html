To create an account you must be at least 16 years old. If you are between 13 and 16, you may use Dishd only with verifiable consent from a parent or legal guardian. Dishd is not directed at children under 13.

4. Community rules

Dishd works because people post food, not fights. The following are not allowed:

  • content you do not have the rights to, including copied recipes and photos taken from other sites;
  • spam, mass-following, fake accounts, scraping and automated posting;
  • hate speech, harassment, threats and content that targets people because of who they are;

We may hide, limit or remove content that breaks these rules and, in repeated or serious cases, suspend or close the account. Where it is reasonable, we tell you what happened and why, and you can appeal by writing to contact@dishd.pl.

You can also report a recipe or an account from inside the app, or by e-mail. Reports about safety are reviewed first.

5. Culinary disclaimer and food safety

Every recipe comes from a community member, not from a test kitchen. Dishd does not verify nutritional values, cooking temperatures, shelf life or the presence of allergens.

Before cooking, check the ingredients yourself — especially if you or someone you cook for has an allergy, intolerance or medical dietary need. When in doubt about food safety, follow official guidance rather than a recipe card.

Recipes are not medical or dietary advice. If you need a diet tailored to a health condition, talk to a doctor or a registered dietitian.
