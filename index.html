What is Dishd

A social cookbook without the clutter

No endless life stories before the ingredients list. Just clean recipe cards, photos from real kitchens, and profiles built around food.

01

Post Your Dishes

Upload photos, ingredients, preparation steps, and tags in seconds so others can recreate your meal.

02

Save & Organize

Bookmark recipes you want to cook later and search instantly by dish name, tags, or @username.

03

Follow Creators

Build your feed around cooks whose taste matches yours, with full control over privacy and blocked accounts.
