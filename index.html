What dishd is

A social cookbook without the clutter

No endless life story before the ingredient list. Just clean recipe cards, photos from real kitchens and profiles built around food.

The idea

Your kitchen. Your rules.

dishd. came from a simple need: a place for recipes that looks good, works with one hand and never gets in the way of cooking. No trackers, no algorithm shouting about reach, no data sold on the side.
