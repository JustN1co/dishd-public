What dishd is

A social cookbook without the clutter

No endless life story before the ingredient list. Just clean recipe cards, photos from real kitchens and profiles built around food.

01

Post your dishes

Add a photo, ingredients, preparation steps and tags in seconds — enough for someone else to cook your meal.

02

Save & organize

Bookmark recipes for later and search instantly by dish name, tag or @handle — no endless scrolling needed.

03

Follow cooks

Build a feed around people whose taste matches yours, with full control over privacy and blocked accounts.

The idea

Your kitchen. Your rules.

dishd. came from a simple need: a place for recipes that looks good, works with one hand and never gets in the way of cooking. No ads, no algorithm shouting about reach, no data sold on the side.

How it works

From your plate to the feed in three steps

Photograph the dish

One phone photo is enough to start a recipe card. No studio, no styling props, no filters required.

Add ingredients & steps

A short, concrete list with the amounts you actually used. Someone else should be able to repeat it.

Publish or save for later

Your dish lands in the feed, and other people's cards are one tap away from your own collection.
