Privacy Policy

Information on how personal data is processed within the Dishd mobile application and the dishd.pl website.

Last updated: 19.11.2026 Domain: dishd.pl Contact: contact@dishd.pl

1. Data Controllers

The joint controllers responsible for processing your personal data in connection with the Dishd application and dishd.pl website are:

Nico Jankowiak & Tomasz Musioł
Ruda Śląska, Poland
E-mail: contact@dishd.pl

2. What Data We Collect

  • Account & authentication data: email address, username (@handle), encrypted password or Google OAuth 2.0 identifier, and two-factor email verification settings.
  • Profile & user-generated content: avatar photo, bio, selected profile banner, published recipes, food photos, tags, likes, saved recipes, and follow/block relationships.
  • Support communications: messages and email addresses provided when contacting us at contact@dishd.pl.

3. Purpose and Legal Basis (GDPR)

  • Service delivery (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR): creating and maintaining your account, signing in, publishing recipes, and syncing saved dishes across devices.
  • Security & legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR): sending two-factor verification codes, preventing spam or abuse, and ensuring technical stability.

4. Infrastructure, Third-Party Services & Data Retention

  • Supabase Inc. — cloud database, user authentication, and storage for profile avatars and recipe photos.
  • Google LLC (Google OAuth 2.0) — optional third-party sign-in. We only access basic profile data (email address, display name, and avatar) required to create and authenticate your Dishd account.
  • Hosting & Transactional Email — infrastructure used to host dishd.pl and deliver account verification / 2FA emails.
  • Data Retention: Your personal data is retained for as long as your account remains active. After account deletion, active records are erased immediately and purged from encrypted backups within 30 days.
  • Cookies & Local Storage: The dishd.pl website does not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies. It only uses technical browser localStorage (dishd_lang) to remember your EN/PL language preference.

5. Your Rights & Account Deletion

Under the GDPR, you have the right to access your personal data, rectify inaccuracies, request erasure ('right to be forgotten'), restrict processing, request data portability, and lodge a complaint with the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO) in Poland.

How to delete your account: You can permanently delete your account directly inside the Dishd app (Profile → Settings → Delete Account) or by sending a deletion request from your registered email address to contact@dishd.pl. All associated profile details, recipes, and uploaded photos will be permanently deleted within 7 days.
