Privacy Policy

This policy explains how personal data is processed in the Dishd mobile application and on the dishd.pl website — what we collect, why we need it, how long we keep it and who we share it with.

Last updated: 19 November 2026 Version: 2.0 Contact: contact@dishd.pl

In short: we collect what an account and a recipe need, we do not sell or profile your data, and you can delete your account at any time.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The joint controllers of the personal data processed in connection with the Dishd application and the dishd.pl website are:

Nico Jankowiak & Tomasz Musioł
Ruda Śląska, Poland
E-mail: contact@dishd.pl

They jointly decide how your data is processed and are jointly responsible for answering your requests under the GDPR. You can contact them about any data matter using the address above.

2. What data we collect

  • Account and authentication data: e-mail address, username (@handle), a password stored in hashed form, or a Google OAuth 2.0 identifier when you sign in with Google, plus your two-step e-mail verification settings.
  • Profile and content you create: avatar, bio, profile banner, published recipes, food photos, tags, likes, saved recipes, and your follow and block relationships.
  • Support correspondence: the content of your message and the e-mail address you write from when you contact us.
  • Basic technical logs: our infrastructure providers may record your IP address, request time and browser type for security and fault diagnosis. We do not use these logs to build profiles and we do not run analytics or advertising scripts.

Providing account and profile data is voluntary, but without it we cannot create an account or let you publish recipes.

3. Purposes and legal bases

  • Providing the service — Article 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of a contract): creating and maintaining your account, signing you in, publishing and displaying recipes, syncing saved dishes across devices, and answering support messages.
  • Security — Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest): sending verification codes, preventing spam, abuse and account takeovers, and keeping the service technically stable. Our legitimate interest is a safe service for everyone who uses it.
  • Legal claims — Article 6(1)(f) GDPR: establishing, pursuing or defending claims connected with the service, for as long as the limitation periods run.

We do not make decisions about you by automated means and we do not profile you for marketing purposes.

4. Who receives your data

We never sell personal data. Access is limited to the processors we need in order to run Dishd, acting on our instructions:

  • Supabase Inc. — database, authentication and file storage for avatars and recipe photos.
  • Google LLC — only if you choose to sign in with Google (Google OAuth 2.0). We receive your e-mail address, display name and avatar, which is the minimum needed to create and authenticate the account. Google Fonts also serves the typefaces used on dishd.pl (see section 6).
  • Hosting and transactional e-mail providers — the infrastructure that serves dishd.pl and delivers verification and two-step codes.

Transfers outside the EEA

Some providers are based in the United States. Where data is transferred outside the European Economic Area, the transfer is based on appropriate safeguards, in particular the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. You can request more information about these safeguards by writing to us.

5. How long we keep your data

  • Account and content: for as long as your account exists.
  • After account deletion: active records — profile, recipes, photos and authentication data — are removed immediately. Copies may remain in encrypted backups for up to 30 days and are not used for any other purpose.
  • Support correspondence: up to 12 months after the matter is closed, so we can pick up the thread if it comes back.
  • Technical logs: a short period set by the infrastructure provider, typically up to 30 days.

6. Cookies, local storage and third-party files

The dishd.pl website does not use advertising or tracking cookies, and there is no consent banner because there is nothing to consent to. The only thing stored in your browser is a technical key, dishd_lang, in local storage — it remembers whether you chose English or Polish.

You can remove it at any time through your browser's site data settings; the site will then fall back to English.

One external file is loaded: the Fraunces and Plus Jakarta Sans typefaces come from Google Fonts. When your browser fetches them, Google receives your IP address and basic request data. If you prefer to avoid this, block fonts.googleapis.com in your browser — the site stays fully readable with system fonts.

7. How we protect your data

Traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Passwords are stored only as hashes, never in plain text. Access to the database is restricted to the people who build Dishd, and two-step e-mail verification is available for every account. Row-level access rules make sure you can only edit your own profile and recipes.

8. Your rights

You have the right to access your data, to receive a copy of it, to correct it, to have it erased, to restrict or object to processing, and to withdraw any consent at any time. We respond within one month of receiving a request.

The full list of rights, the exact procedure and the complaint route to the Polish data protection authority (UODO) are described on the GDPR page.

Read the GDPR information

9. Changes to this policy

When the app changes in a way that affects your data, we update this page and the date at the top. Significant changes — such as a new category of data or a new processor — are announced in the app before they take effect.

Questions about this policy? Write to contact@dishd.pl — a person, not a ticket queue, reads it.
