Privacy Policy

This policy explains how personal data is processed in the Dishd mobile application and on the dishd.pl website — what we collect, why we need it, how long we keep it and who we share it with.

Effective from: 23 November 2026 Version: 2.2 Contact: contact@dishd.pl

In short: we collect only what an account, recipes and notifications need, we never sell your data, and you can export or delete your account at any time.

1. Data controllers

The joint controllers of personal data processed in the Dishd application and on the dishd.pl website are Nico Jankowiak and Tomasz Musioł, Ruda Śląska, Poland. Contact for all data matters: contact@dishd.pl. The joint controllers jointly determine how personal data is processed and are jointly responsible for responding to requests under the GDPR.

2. What data we collect

Data Source Purpose
E-mail address, handle, password (stored strictly as a hash) Provided at registration Signing in and account management
Google (OAuth) or Apple (Sign in with Apple) identifier, e-mail and name from the provider account When you choose Google/Apple sign-in Creating and authenticating your account
Handle, display name, bio, profile photo, profile banner, website link Provided in the app Profile visible to other users
Recipes: title, description, ingredients, steps, tags, photos, videos Added in the app Publishing recipes

Dietary preferences, allergies, favourite cuisines, muted words, privacy settings Provided in the app Personalisation and safety
Content and account reports (reason, description, related recipe/user) Submitted in the app Content moderation
Push notification token (Expo Push Token), app language, device model in provider logs Your device, once notifications are enabled Delivering notifications
Basic technical logs (IP address, request timestamp, client type) Supabase / hosting infrastructure Security and diagnostics
Date and version of terms acceptance, age confirmation Registration / acceptance screen Proof of consent and legal compliance

The app accesses your camera and photo library only when you choose a photo (dish photo, avatar, collection cover). We do not record audio, and we do not collect location data, contacts or advertising identifiers. Language, theme and appearance settings are stored solely on your device.

Photos are publicly accessible via URL. Files in the avatars and recipes buckets have public URLs, so anyone who knows the address can view the photo — including photos from a private profile or a recipe visible only to followers. The URL is neither indexed nor publicly listed, but you should not treat it as secret. Deleting a photo or your account makes the file unavailable at that URL; cached copies on the CDN may take up to 24 hours to clear.

3. Legal bases (GDPR)

  • Article 6(1)(b) GDPR — performance of a contract: maintaining your account, publishing and displaying content, syncing saved recipes, and handling support requests and complaints.
  • Article 6(1)(a) GDPR — consent: push notifications (you enable them yourself in Settings and can turn them off at any time), as well as future features for which we ask separately.
  • Article 6(1)(f) GDPR — legitimate interest: security, preventing spam and account takeovers, moderating reported content, and establishing, pursuing or defending legal claims.

We do not make decisions about you solely by automated means, nor do we build profiles that produce legal effects.

4. Who receives your data

We do not sell personal data and we do not share it with data brokers. Access is restricted strictly to processors acting on our documented instructions:

  • Supabase Inc. — database, authentication and file storage (avatars, recipe photos);
  • Google LLC — only when you choose to sign in with Google (OAuth 2.0); we receive your e-mail address, display name and profile photo;
  • Apple Inc. — only when you choose “Sign in with Apple”; we receive an identifier, e-mail address (or its encrypted relay equivalent) and name, if you share it;
  • Expo / Google Firebase Cloud Messaging — delivering push notifications (device token and notification content);
  • Hosting and transactional e-mail providers — serving dishd.pl and sending verification and two-step sign-in codes.

Transfers outside the EEA

Some providers are based in the United States. Transfers take place on the basis of the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and additional provider safeguards. You can receive a summary of these safeguards by e-mail.

5. How long we keep your data

  • Account, profile and published content — until you delete the content or your account.
  • After account deletion, active records (profile, recipes, photos, login data, likes, follows, blocks, push tokens, reports submitted by you) are removed immediately; files in Storage are deleted by the app and additionally swept by a nightly cleanup job.
  • Encrypted backups — up to 30 days, solely for service recovery purposes.
  • Support correspondence — up to 12 months after the matter is closed.
  • Technical logs — for the period set by the provider, typically up to 30 days.

6. Your rights

You have the right to access your data, receive a copy of it, rectify it, erase it, restrict processing, data portability, object to processing, and withdraw consent at any time (without affecting processing prior to withdrawal). We respond within one month; in complex cases we may extend this period by two months, notifying you accordingly.

You can lodge a complaint with the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warsaw, Poland, uodo.gov.pl).

Account deletion: in the app via Profile → Settings → Delete account, through https://dishd.pl/delete-account.html, or by e-mailing contact@dishd.pl from the address linked to your account. You can also export your data in the app: Settings → Privacy & security → Download my data.

Read the GDPR information

7. Children

Dishd is intended for people aged 16 and over. Younger individuals may use the app only with the consent of a parent or legal guardian, and the app is not directed at children under 13. You confirm your age during registration and when signing in for the first time with Google or Apple. If you suspect an account belongs to a child under 16 without the required consent, write to contact@dishd.pl — we will delete the account and associated data.

8. Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Passwords are stored strictly as hashes. Database access is restricted by Row Level Security rules: you can edit only your own profile and your own content. Two-step sign-in verification codes are available for every account.

9. Changes to this policy

When changes affect your data, we notify you in the app and update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes — a new category of data or a new processor — are announced in the app before taking effect. The current version is always available on dishd.pl. Questions can be sent to contact@dishd.pl.

10. Cookies and local storage

The dishd.pl website sets no cookies and loads no external tracking scripts or third-party font embeds. In browser local storage we save only the technical key dishd_lang (your selected language), and in the mobile app we store only local language, theme and appearance settings on your device.
