GDPR / RODO

A practical summary of your data protection rights at dishd, the legal bases we rely on, and exactly how to send a request — with the timelines you can expect.

In short: one e-mail to contact@dishd.pl is enough to exercise any right. Requests are free, we answer within one month, and we never ask for more data than we need to confirm it is you.

1. Data controller and contact

The joint controllers of your personal data are Nico Jankowiak and Tomasz Musioł, Ruda Śląska, Poland. For anything related to personal data — a question, a request or a complaint — write to contact@dishd.pl.

We have not appointed a Data Protection Officer, because the processing we carry out does not meet the criteria that make one mandatory. Data matters are handled directly by the controllers.

2. Scope of processing

Dishd processes the data needed to run an account-based recipe app: identification and login data, profile details, recipes and photos you publish, likes, saves and follow relationships, support messages, and short-lived technical logs kept by our infrastructure providers.

The detailed catalogue, including every category and its source, is in section 2 of the privacy policy.

See what data we collect

3. Legal bases

  • Article 6(1)(b) GDPR — contract: creating and maintaining your account, publishing and displaying recipes, syncing saved dishes, and handling support.
  • Article 6(1)(f) GDPR — legitimate interest: security, fraud and spam prevention, moderation of reported content, technical stability and defending legal claims.
  • Article 6(1)(a) GDPR — consent: used only where we explicitly ask for it, for example if a future feature requires it. You can withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing carried out beforehand.

We do not carry out profiling that produces legal effects, and we do not make automated decisions about you.

4. Recipients and transfers outside the EEA

Processors: Supabase Inc. (database, authentication, file storage), Google LLC (only when you sign in with Google, and for the web fonts on dishd.pl), our hosting provider and our transactional e-mail provider. Each one processes data only on our documented instructions.

Where data leaves the European Economic Area, the transfer relies on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses together with the provider's supplementary security measures. You can ask for a summary of those safeguards by e-mail.

We do not sell data and we do not share it with data brokers.

5. Retention periods

  • Account, profile and published recipes — until you delete the account or the specific content.
  • Encrypted backups — up to 30 days after deletion, then purged automatically.
  • Support correspondence — up to 12 months after the matter is closed.
  • Technical logs — set by the infrastructure provider, typically up to 30 days.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the following rights, and we do not charge for exercising them:

  • Access (Article 15): confirmation of whether we process your data, plus a copy and information about how it is used.
  • Rectification (Article 16): correction of inaccurate data and completion of incomplete data — most profile fields you can edit yourself in the app.
  • Erasure (Article 17): the right to be forgotten, exercised by deleting your account or asking us to remove specific content.
  • Restriction (Article 18): we keep the data but stop using it while a question about its accuracy or lawfulness is resolved.
  • Portability (Article 20): a machine-readable export of the data you provided to us, based on contract or consent.
  • Objection (Article 21): object to processing based on our legitimate interest — we then stop, unless we can show compelling grounds that override your rights.
  • Withdrawing consent (Article 7): withdraw any consent you have given, at any time, without giving a reason.

7. How to send a request

Send an e-mail from the address registered to your account to contact@dishd.pl, with the subject line chosen from the request types below. If you write from a different address, we may ask you to confirm the account so that nobody else can act on it.

  1. Data access / copy — we send a summary and export of your data.
  2. Correction — tell us which field is wrong and what it should be.
  3. Deletion — see the next section for the fastest route.
  4. Restriction or objection — describe which processing you want limited and why.

We respond within one month of receiving the request. If the request is complex or we receive many of them, we may extend this by up to two further months — we will tell you within the first month and explain why. If we cannot identify you from the message, the clock starts once you confirm your identity.

8. Deleting your account

The fastest route is in the app: Profile → Settings → Delete account. You can also write from your registered address to contact@dishd.pl and we will do it for you.

  • Active records — profile, recipes, photos and login data — are erased immediately.
  • Encrypted backups are purged within 30 days and are not used for anything else in the meantime.
  • Deletion is permanent. Exported copies you made yourself, and content other users screenshot or saved, are outside our control.

9. Complaint to the supervisory authority

If you believe we process your data unlawfully, tell us first — most issues are solved with one e-mail. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, UODO):

Urząd Ochrony Danych Osobowych
ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warsaw, Poland
Website: uodo.gov.pl

Complaints can be filed in Polish or in writing through the UODO website. You can also turn to a consumer organisation or, in cross-border cases, to the supervisory authority in your country of residence.
